Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

1. Overview

SupaWake ("we", "us", "our") is a free, open-source web application that helps developers keep their Supabase free-tier projects active. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use SupaWake, how we use it, and how we protect it.

By using SupaWake, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

2. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication receives your Google account name, email address, and profile photo URL. We do not store your password — authentication is handled entirely by Google.

Project data. When you add a Supabase project, we store the following in Firebase Firestore, linked to your account:

  • Your Supabase project URL (e.g. https://xyzabc.supabase.co)
  • Your Supabase anon key (the public API key — not your service_role key)
  • An optional label you provide for the project
  • Timestamps of when projects were added and last pinged
  • Project status (Awake, Pending, or Error)

We do not collect passwords, payment information, location data, device identifiers, or any information beyond what is listed above.

3. How we use your information

We use the information you provide solely to deliver the SupaWake service:

  • Your Google account ID is used to keep your projects private and separate from other users' data.
  • Your Supabase project URL and anon key are used exclusively to send keepalive requests to your database every 5 days (and when you trigger a manual ping).
  • Timestamps are used to display last pinged times in your dashboard and to enforce the once-per-day rate limit on manual pings.

We do not sell your data, share it with third parties for marketing, or use it for any purpose beyond operating the SupaWake service.

4. Data storage and security

Your project data is stored in Firebase Firestore, a cloud database operated by Google. Access is protected by Firestore Security Rules that enforce the following: only the authenticated user who created a project document can read, update, or delete it. No other user — including us — can access your project data through the application interface.

The automated pinging is performed by a GitHub Actions workflow that reads project data from Firestore using a service account with read-only access to the projects collection.

While we implement reasonable security measures, no system is perfectly secure. We encourage you to never store sensitive credentials (such as your Supabase service_role key) in SupaWake.

5. Third-party services

SupaWake uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and database storage. Subject to Firebase's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Actions — automated scheduled pinging. Subject to GitHub's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdSense — advertising displayed on the site. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to SupaWake or other sites. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out at adssettings.google.com.

6. Cookies

SupaWake itself does not set cookies directly. Firebase Authentication uses browser storage (IndexedDB or localStorage) to maintain your login session across visits. Google AdSense may set cookies for advertising purposes as described in section 5.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained in Firestore for as long as your account exists. You can delete individual projects at any time from your dashboard. To request full deletion of your account and all associated data, contact us at the email address below. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

8. Children's privacy

SupaWake is intended for developers and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of SupaWake after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request data deletion, contact us via the SupaWake GitHub repository or at the email address listed there.
